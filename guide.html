Spatial UI & Emotion Workflow Guide / یوزر مینوئل

Spatial UI & Emotion Steps:

  • Step 1 (Setup): Enter business and customer information easily inside floating 3D glass frames.
  • Step 2 (Items): Manage individual item pricing or single lump-sum descriptions seamlessly.
  • Step 3 (Finance): Handle tax rates, payment status (Paid/Udhar), and deposit balances.
  • Step 4 (Signatures): Apply ink color filters and official digital rubber stamps.
