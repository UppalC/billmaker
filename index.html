Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Feature Highlights

Everything you need to know about BillMaker Pro Studio - Zero Installation, 100% Free & Secure.

Is my business data safe with BillMaker Pro?

Yes! BillMaker Pro Studio is a serverless application. All your invoices, settings, and signature data remain stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage. No data is ever transmitted to an external server.

Does it support Thermal POS Printers?

Absolutely! You can switch seamlessly between standard A4 paper, 80mm POS thermal rolls, and 58mm compact thermal receipt sizes with optimized single-column print layouts.

How does the Amount in Words feature work?

The embedded calculation engine automatically translates numerical Grand Totals into clear written English text (e.g., "Rupees Only") adapted to your selected currency symbol.

How does auto-incrementing work?

When you click "Print Invoice" or "Download PDF", the invoice sequence number (e.g., INV-1098) automatically updates to the next number (INV-1099) for your next transaction.

Privacy Policy

BillMaker Pro Studio is built with a zero-server privacy architecture. Your business names, customer contacts, prices, and uploaded signatures are stored exclusively in your browser's LocalStorage.

No data is uploaded to external servers, ensuring 100% confidentiality for your business accounting.
