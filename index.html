BillMaker Pro User Guide / Manual

English Manual:

  • Document & Paper: Switch between Tax Invoice / Quotation and A4, 58mm, or 80mm thermal rolls instantly.
  • Invoice Number: Auto-increments automatically on print/save, or click 'Auto' / type custom numbers manually.
  • Pricing & Items: Add individual items with quantity/price or switch to Lump-sum single price mode.
